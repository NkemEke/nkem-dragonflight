INTRODUCTION

My background

My name is Nkemakolam Eke and I am from Edmonton, Alberta. I was born in Nigeria as the second child in a family with five children. I am married to a physician colleague, and we have three children (two teenage boys and a 10-year-old girl). I am in my 22nd year of practice as a medical doctor. I had attended medical school in Nigeria which I started at the age of 17 years and later specialized in obstetrics and gynecology with special interest in reproductive endocrinology and assisted reproduction. I worked as a consultant obstetrician and gynecologist and fertility specialist at a federal tertiary hospital for a few years. In 2015, I relocated to Canada with my wife and children and had since worked as a family physician (with obstetrics). While in Canada, I have obtained the Canadian board certification in family medicine.

I have been actively involved in teaching of medical students, interns and resident doctors for the past 17 years and I am currently an Associate Clinical Professor and the International Medical Graduate [IMG] Co-Director for Family Medicine Residency Training at the Faculty of Medicine and Dentistry of the University of Alberta. I am also involved in various postgraduate training academic and leadership committees in my department including Residency Program Committee, Residency Operations Committee and Residency Curriculum Committee.

I have also served as an assessor with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA) for Practice Ready Physicians who are seeking independent medical practice licensure in Alberta. I am currently an elected council member at the CPSA and chair elect of Governance committee for the council.

I have been actively involved in clinical research and trials, peer review of scientific papers, and serving as editor of some medical journals. In the past, I had served as Associate Editor of Nigerian Journal of Medicine and Editor-in-chief of Afrimedic Journal. I currently serve as Deputy Editor of Canpad Medical Journal and International Editor of the Tropical Journal of Medical Research. I am also a reviewer of medical research works for journals.

I am also a member many national and international professional bodies, including Canadian Association of Nigerian Physicians and Dentists, in which I am a licensed professional member of the Continued Medical Education committee of which part of the work comprises teaching and preceptorship, curriculum development and program evaluation.

Outside academics, I like singing, watching movies and travelling. I also make out time to follow up with community works at my ancestral hometown where my father is the ruling king.

Reason for Master of Education Program

I have always strived for excellence and felt that I might need to do additional learning to ensure I am giving the best learning experience to my medical students and resident doctors. I also wanted to contribute positively to the educational processes in my department and faculty, and acquisition of additional advanced knowledge in education would help me make such contributions in an effective and competent manner. I also understood that undertaking the program would improve my experience with teaching and expand my knowledge in another realm that goes beyond clinical practice.

My Master of Education Journey

My experience during this Master of Education (major in Health Professions Education) journey has been interesting as I have been faced regularly in my clinical teaching career with occasions requiring application of the knowledge I have acquired from the program. I noticed that my knowledge and teaching capabilities kept improving as I advanced in the program. I run a very busy clinical practice alongside my work at the University of Alberta and College of Physicians and Surgeons of Alberta. I have been faced quite frequently with time availability difficulties due to my other duties. I had to make extra time and sacrifice, keeping awake at odd hours to complete the required reading and assignments.

The completion of my Master of Education program has provided me with a comprehensive opportunity to develop advanced academic, professional, interpersonal, and leadership competencies that extend beyond disciplinary knowledge and contribute meaningfully to my effectiveness as a clinical educator. The twelve courses I undertook throughout the program collectively strengthened my capacity to engage critically with educational challenges, develop innovative solutions, manage complex projects, communicate effectively, foster collaborative relationships, and provide ethical and transformational leadership. These learning experiences align closely with the transferrable professional skills identified by the University of Saskatchewan's College of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies (CGPS). The courses complemented one another in developing my capacity to understand educational systems, lead instructional improvement, manage organizational processes, design and evaluate educational programs, facilitate meaningful learning, conduct scholarly inquiry, and make responsible professional decisions. Through theoretical engagement, critical reflection, scholarly inquiry, collaborative learning, and the application of educational concepts to practical contexts, I progressively developed competencies that are transferable across educational institutions, professional environments, and broader organizational settings.

In conclusion, this Master of Education program has contributed significantly to my transformation into a more reflective, analytical, innovative, collaborative, and ethically responsible clinical educator. The knowledge and competencies acquired through this program have strengthened my readiness to assume responsibilities that require strategic thinking, professional judgement, effective communication, organizational coordination, and transformational leadership. Notably, these acquired skills can be applied to my primary workplace as well as other educational institutions, government agencies, professional organizations, research environments, and other sectors requiring effective leadership and organizational development. My Master of Education learning journey has therefore represented my acquisition of advanced educational knowledge and development of a comprehensive professional skill set that positions me to contribute meaningfully to institutional improvement, educational innovation, inclusive learning environments, and broader societal development.
